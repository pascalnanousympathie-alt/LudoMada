## Service
Mada Commander is intended to provide authenticated access to diagnostics and status for a user's own explicitly linked developer workspace. Features available in the public version may be more limited than the private self-hosted Commander.

## Account and device ownership
Users may connect only devices, repositories, and workspaces they own or are authorized to administer. Users must keep their sign-in and pairing credentials private and revoke lost or unused connections.

## Acceptable use
Users may not attempt cross-account access, bypass workspace isolation, use the service to access another person's data, or submit abusive requests. The public service is not intended to provide unrestricted shell access or arbitrary destructive file operations.

## Availability and risks
Development tools can report incorrect or incomplete diagnostic results. Users should review generated changes and maintain backups before applying modifications. Any production implementation must clearly state which operations are supported and how confirmation works.

## Termination and changes
The local website includes an account-deletion action that removes the Mada Commander public-service account and its linked service records.
